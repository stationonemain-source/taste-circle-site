Support

Quick answers are below. If you need anything else, email us and someone on the TasteCircle team will get back to you.

Contact us

Email: tastecirclemain@gmail.com

If something isn't working, include your username, your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it went wrong. A screenshot helps a lot.

Restaurant partnerships, press and everything else go to the same inbox.

Delete your account

You can delete your account from inside the app:

  • Go to your Profile and tap the gear icon to open Settings.
  • Tap Delete Account.
  • Follow the prompts to confirm.

This permanently deletes your account and profile, your posts and photos, your comments and likes, and your friend connections. Your Crumbs, rank and rewards are deleted too. This can't be undone. If you signed in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm once, so that we can also disconnect your Apple ID from TasteCircle.

If you can't get into the app, email tastecirclemain@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my account", and we'll delete it for you and let you know when it's done.

To find out what happens to your data after you delete your account, see our Privacy Policy.

Report a post, comment or person

  • To report a post: open the post's menu (the dots on the post), tap Report, and choose a reason. Reports go to the TasteCircle team for review.
  • When you report a post, that person's posts are also hidden from your feed.
  • To report a comment: tap the dots next to the comment and choose Report comment.
  • To report a person, a Table, or something said in a Table chat, or to undo a hide, email us with the username involved. There isn't an in-app button for these yet.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

Can I buy Crumbs?

No. Crumbs can't be bought, and there are no in-app purchases in TasteCircle. You earn Crumbs by using the app, for example by completing daily quests like posting a plate and by finishing Table challenges. You can use them for rewards and punch cards at partner restaurants by tapping your phone on the restaurant's TasteCircle tag.

What are Tables?

Tables are private groups, like your roommates, your team or your club. You join a Table with its QR code or 5-digit code. Posts and chat inside a Table are visible only to its members, and they don't appear in your main feed. Each Table can run challenges and has its own leaderboard. Anyone can start a Table.

Do I have to use the nutrition features?

No. Nutrition tracking is completely optional. Your food diary is private to you, and nutrition details show on a post only if you choose to add them. Calorie and macro numbers are available only to people 18 and older. You can pause or turn off the nutrition features in Settings. TasteCircle isn't medical or nutrition advice. If you're struggling with food or eating, the National Eating Disorders Association has free resources.

Who can see my posts?

Your friends see your posts in your Inner Circle feed. Other TasteCircle users can see them in the Outer Circle feed and in search. To keep your posts to your friends only, turn on Private Posts in Settings. Posts you share in a Table stay inside that Table.

Is TasteCircle free?

Yes. TasteCircle is free on the iPhone App Store. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

Why does TasteCircle ask for my location or contacts?

Location helps you tag the restaurant you're eating at and find places and deals nearby. Contacts let you text an invite to friends from your own Messages app. We don't upload your address book. Both are optional, and you can change either one anytime in your iPhone's Settings.

The app says I need to update

From time to time we need everyone to be on a newer version. Get the latest version of TasteCircle from the App Store, then open the app again.

Privacy Policy

Taste Circle, LLC

Effective date: February 5, 2026. Last updated: September 25, 2026.

This policy explains what information the TasteCircle app and tastecircle.org collect, how we use it, who can see it, and the choices you have. "TasteCircle", "we" and "us" mean Taste Circle, LLC, a Texas limited liability company. If you have a question about anything here, email tastecirclemain@gmail.com.

The short version

  • By default, your posts are seen by your friends and can also be seen by other TasteCircle users. You can keep your posts to your friends with the Private Posts setting.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • The app doesn't show ads.
  • Location, contacts, camera, photos, microphone and Apple Health are used only if you allow them on your phone, and you can turn each one off in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Nutrition tracking is optional, and your food diary is private to you.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address and password, or your phone number if you sign in by phone. If you use Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the name and email address that service shares with us.
  • Your date of birth, which we ask for at sign-up to check that you are old enough to use TasteCircle.
  • Your profile: username, display name, profile photo and bio.
  • What you post: photos, captions, ratings, reviews, the places you tag, comments, likes, reposts and posts you save.
  • Tables: the Tables you create or join, and the posts and chat messages you share in them.
  • Friends: friend requests you send and receive, your friends list, and the people whose posts you've hidden by reporting them.
  • Reports, feedback-board posts, and anything you send us by email.
  • Nutrition information, if you choose to use the nutrition features (see "Nutrition features" below).

Information collected when you use the app

  • How you use the app: which screens you open and actions such as posting, liking, commenting, searching and sending friend requests. We collect this with our analytics provider, PostHog, and it is linked to your account.
  • Device and app information: device model, iOS version, app version, IP address, and technical identifiers we use to keep the service secure and to send you notifications.
  • Rewards activity: your Crumbs balance and history, XP, Taste Rank, achievements, streaks, challenge progress, and punches or rewards you collect at partner restaurants.

Permissions on your phone

The app asks before using any of these. Saying no only switches off the feature that needs it.

  • Location: to find restaurants near you when you tag a post, and to show nearby deals. We use it while you're using the app. The place you tag (its name and map location) is saved with your post. You can still post without sharing your location.
  • Camera and microphone: to take photos for your posts. In app version 1.2.1 and later, the microphone can also be used to log food by voice. Voice logging uses Apple's speech recognition, and TasteCircle doesn't store the recording.
  • Photo library: to choose photos to post, and to save photos to your library when you ask.
  • Contacts: to let you text an invite link to friends. Your contacts are read on your phone to show the list, and the invite is sent from your own Messages app. We don't upload your address book.
  • NFC: to tap a partner restaurant's TasteCircle tag to collect a punch or redeem Crumbs. We record which partner you tapped and when.
  • Notifications: to tell you about things like friend requests, comments, likes, Table activity and reminders. We store a notification token for your device.
  • Apple Health (app version 1.2.1 and later, optional): if you connect it, we read your steps and active calories to show them next to your food diary. This happens on your phone. We don't write anything to Apple Health, and we don't store or share your Apple Health data.

Nutrition features

  • The nutrition features are optional. If you use them, we store your food diary (foods, amounts, meals and any meal photos), goals you set, weight entries if you log them, and your settings for these features, including the date of birth you give us to confirm your age.
  • Calorie and macro numbers are offered only to people 18 and older.
  • Your food diary is private to you. Nutrition details appear on a post only if you choose to show them on that post.
  • When you search for a food or scan a barcode, we send the search words or barcode to USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts to look up nutrition information. We don't send your name or account details with that lookup.
  • Nothing in TasteCircle is medical or nutrition advice. See our Terms of Service.

How we use information

  • To run TasteCircle: your account, your feeds, Tables, search, friend suggestions and notifications.
  • To run the rewards: counting Crumbs, XP and ranks, checking redemptions at partner restaurants, and stopping cheating.
  • To personalize what you see, such as friend suggestions, trending posts and Taste DNA.
  • To keep people safe: reviewing reports, enforcing our rules, and blocking spam and bots (for example, on phone sign-in).
  • To understand how the app is used and make it better.
  • To contact you about your account and, from time to time, about TasteCircle news. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To follow the law and protect the rights and safety of our users and the company.

Who can see what

  • Your username, display name and profile photo can be seen by other TasteCircle users, for example in search and friend suggestions.
  • Your posts: your friends see them in your Inner Circle feed. Other TasteCircle users can see them in the Outer Circle feed and in search. To keep your posts to your friends only, turn on Private Posts in Settings.
  • Tables: posts and chat messages in a Table are visible only to that Table's members, and Table posts don't appear in the main feed.
  • Your food diary is visible only to you.
  • The Bite (a daily game): if you turn on "Feature my plates" (The Bite) in Settings (off by default, 18 and over only), one of your public, restaurant-tagged posts may be shown to all players as the day's puzzle, with your username, the month you posted, your caption (unless it names the place) and the restaurant. Table posts and posts from a private profile are never used. We keep the date of birth you give for this only while the setting is on. Your game scores are visible only to you unless you share them.
  • The TasteCircle team can see content when we need to, for example to review a report.

Who we share information with

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only in these cases:

  • With service providers that run parts of TasteCircle for us:
    • Google Firebase: sign-in, our database, photo storage, server functions, push notifications, and bot protection.
    • PostHog: product analytics.
    • Apple and Google: if you choose Sign in with Apple or Google, and for Apple's speech recognition and translation features.
    • Google Translate: to translate captions when Apple's translation isn't available on your device.
    • Apple Maps and Foursquare: to find places near you. We send your approximate position to find nearby places.
    • USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts: for food search and barcode lookups.
    • PurgoMalum: to check new usernames for offensive words. Only the username is sent.
  • With other TasteCircle users, as described in "Who can see what".
  • With partner restaurants, only as far as needed to honor a reward or punch you redeem with them.
  • When the law requires it, or when we need to protect the rights, property or safety of TasteCircle, our users or the public.
  • If TasteCircle is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, subject to confidentiality protections.

Advertising and tracking

The app doesn't show ads. We don't use Apple's advertising identifier, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How long we keep information, and deleting your account

We keep your information while your account is open.

You can delete your account in the app at any time: go to your Profile, tap the gear icon to open Settings, tap Delete Account, and confirm. This permanently deletes your account and profile, your posts and photos, your comments and likes, your friend connections, and the rewards and nutrition data stored with your account. If you can't get into the app, email tastecirclemain@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my account" and we'll do it for you. More help is on our Support page.

Deletion requests are usually completed within 30 days. Copies in backups may remain for up to 90 days. We may keep some information for longer when the law requires it, to resolve disputes, or to enforce our rules. For example, if an account is banned for breaking our rules, we keep its email address so it can't be used to sign up again.

Your choices and rights

  • You can edit your profile, turn Private Posts on or off, and manage notifications in the app.
  • You can turn off location, contacts, camera, photos, microphone, notifications and Apple Health for TasteCircle in your iPhone's Settings.
  • You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any of them.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, California or the European Economic Area), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict some uses of it, and to receive a copy of it. To make a request, email tastecirclemain@gmail.com. We may need to confirm it's you before we act on it.

Children and teens

TasteCircle is for people who are 16 or older, the minimum age in our Terms. When you create an account you confirm that you're 16 or older. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If we learn that we have, we'll delete it. A parent or guardian who believes their child is using TasteCircle can email us to have the account removed. Calorie and macro tracking is available only to people 18 and older.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, such as limiting who can access our database. No system is completely secure, so we can't guarantee the security of your information. If a data breach affects your personal information, we'll notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

Where information is processed

TasteCircle is based in the United States. We and our service providers may process your information in the United States and in other countries.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we'll post the new version here and change the date at the top. If we make a significant change, we'll let you know in the app or by email.
